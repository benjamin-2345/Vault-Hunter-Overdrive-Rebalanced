## 🧭 The Philosophy Behind the Vaultkeeper

Most people approach inventory utilities as if they were a wrench — grab it, twist the bolt, move on. The Vaultkeeper was born from a different instinct entirely. We looked at the sprawling, chaotic beauty of a long-played character's stash and saw something worth *honoring*. Mountains of gear, each with a story. Forgotten builds waiting to be rediscovered. Precious artifacts buried beneath a hundred incidental drops.

The Sanctuary Vaultkeeper Companion exists to turn that chaos into a **curated gallery**. It does not impose order — it *reveals* the order that was always latent within your collection. Through intelligent tagging, visual clustering, and narrative-driven categorization, your inventory becomes something you can wander through with pride.

The name "Vaultkeeper" itself is a promise: whatever the boundarylands throw at you, there will always be a keeper standing guard over what matters.

---

## ✨ What Makes This Project Distinct

There are countless utilities in the wider ecosystem, so why pour hundreds of hours into another one? Because the Vaultkeeper is built on four convictions that are rarely combined:

1. **Preservation over replacement.** We never touch your original save data in a destructive way. Everything is versioned, sandboxed, and reversible.
2. **Clarity over cleverness.** A tool should feel obvious. If a feature needs a tutorial, it needs a redesign.
3. **Community over corporate.** Every translation, every theme, every plugin is contributed by players, for players.
4. **Longevity over novelty.** We architect for 2026 and beyond — no flash-in-the-pan frameworks. Stable, boring, dependable tech underneath a delightful surface.

## 🎛️ Feature Constellation

Below is the current feature set, grouped by intent. Each entry represents a substantial subsystem, not a checkbox.

### 🗂️ Collection Curation
- Intelligent tagging engine with fuzzy matching and manual override
- Visual "shelf" views that group items by aesthetic era, elemental affinity, or playstyle archetype
- Wishlist tracking with soft reminders when relevant items appear in your logs
- Build snapshot system: save the exact loadout you used for a memorable run and recall it later

### 🔄 Synchronization Engine
- Bidirectional profile mirroring across multiple local vaults
- Conflict resolution wizard that explains *why* two versions diverged
- Encrypted local backup rotation with configurable retention windows
- Timeline replay: scrub backward and forward through the history of a single item

### 🌐 Multilingual Support Framework
- Ten shipped locales at launch, with a community portal for new languages
- Right-to-left rendering parity for the languages that need it
- Locale-aware number and date formatting in every table, chart, and label
- Live language switching without a restart

### 📱 Responsive Interface
- Adaptive layout that respects anything from a pocket-sized tablet to an ultrawide monitor
- Keyboard-first navigation for power users; touch gestures for casual ones
- Dark, light, sepia, and high-contrast themes, all WCAG-aware
- Reduced-motion mode that preserves every functional affordance

### 🔐 Safety & Privacy
- Zero telemetry by default — nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly sync to your own storage
- Local-only credential vault using OS-native keystores
- Plugin permission sandbox so third-party extensions declare exactly what they touch
- Signed release artifacts with published checksums

### 🧩 Extensibility
- Public plugin API with TypeScript definitions and inline documentation
- Theme marketplace pipeline (community-driven, moderated)
- Scriptable "keeper rules" that let automation lovers orchestrate their own workflows
- Headless mode for those who want to drive the Companion from other tooling

## 🧪 A Guided Tour of the Modules

### The Atrium
The Atrium is the Companion's landing space. Rather than a wall of raw numbers, it presents a **narrative dashboard**: recently curated items, pending decisions, and gentle suggestions inspired by your own habits. It is the reception desk of your personal vault.

### The Loom
The Loom is where categorization happens. Items flow in as raw threads; the Loom weaves them into fabrics of meaning. Whether you prefer strict taxonomies or looser folksonomic tags, the Loom adapts.

### The Chronoscope
Time is the most underrated dimension of any collection. The Chronoscope lets you view your vault as it existed last week, last month, or during a specific memorable session. Ideal for understanding your own evolving taste.

### The Forum
Named not for internet forums but for the Roman *forum* — a gathering place — this module hosts internal discussions between you and your future self. Leave notes on items, set reminders, annotate builds with the reasoning behind them.

### The Bridge
Every serious project eventually needs to connect to the outside world. The Bridge handles optional integration with self-hosted storage, cloud sync providers you personally trust, and export formats for external analysis.

## 📐 Responsive Interface & Accessibility

A tool that only works on one type of screen is a tool that fails most of its users. Our interface team treats responsiveness as a first-class requirement, not an afterthought:

- **Fluid grids** that recompose themselves based on available pixels rather than hard-coded breakpoints
- **Container queries** so components self-manage layout regardless of where they are embedded
- **Semantic HTML** that screen readers can traverse confidently
- **Focus visibility** that never disappears, no matter the theme
- **Color contrast** audited against WCAG 2.2 AA and beyond
- **Locale-agnostic typography** that respects scripts with different vertical metrics

The result is an interface that feels native whether you are curled up with a small laptop or presiding over a multi-monitor command center.

## 🌍 Multilingual Support Framework

Language is not a feature; it is a doorway. From the very first commit, the Vaultkeeper has been designed as a **localization-first** application. Every string is externalized, every layout has been battle-tested against the longest plausible translation, and every contributor gets a friendly onboarding guide for adding a new locale.

Currently shipped locales include English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian, Polish, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese. Community efforts are underway for Turkish, Ukrainian, and Vietnamese — check the localization portal for progress and to lend a hand.

Language packs are versioned independently, so a translator's improvement to a phrase does not require a full application release.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Curiosities

**Does this project modify my original game data?**
No. The Companion works on copies and on its own companion store. Your originals remain untouched.

**Is there a mobile version?**
The responsive interface makes the desktop Companion usable on tablets, and a dedicated companion app for handheld screens is on the long-term roadmap.

**Can I contribute without writing code?**
Absolutely. Translators, designers, technical writers, moderators, and bug triagers are all as valuable as engineers.

**How do I keep my collection safe if my machine fails?**
The backup rotation system encrypts and stores local snapshots, and the optional Bridge module can mirror them to storage you control.

**Where do I request a feature?**
The discussion board has a dedicated "Ideas" area where community votes help prioritize the roadmap.
